## Gate hours and facility access
The facility gates are open every day from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM, including weekends and public holidays. The office is staffed Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Your personal gate code works only during gate hours. If you need after-hours access, contact the office in advance so we can arrange it.

## Adding an authorized access contact
You can add a second person as an authorized access contact for your unit. Send us their full name and phone number and we will add them to your unit's gate profile and issue them their own gate code. For security, the account holder must make the request.

## Gate fobs and lost fobs
Each unit comes with one gate fob. You can request a second fob from the office for a one-off fee of $15. If you lose a fob, tell us straight away: we will deactivate it and issue a replacement for $15.

## Temporary gate codes for movers
We can create a temporary gate code for a moving crew or helper. Tell us the date and time window you need, and the code will work only during that window. Please request it at least one business day in advance.

## Insurance add-on billing
The optional tenant insurance add-on is billed monthly together with your rent, on the same autopay date, and shown as a separate line on your statement. Coverage limits depend on the plan you chose at move-in. You can change or cancel the add-on at any time; the change applies from your next billing cycle.

## Payments, autopay and statements
Autopay charges your card or bank account on your billing date each month. Monthly statements and your full payment history are available in your account portal, and we can email you an itemized statement or a copy of a past statement on request.

## Late payments and grace period
There is a 5-day grace period after your billing date. If a payment is still outstanding after the grace period, a late fee of $20 is added. If you are having trouble paying, contact us before the due date: we can set up a payment plan. Units are only ever at risk of lien or auction after repeated missed payments and written notices.

## Unit transfers, upsizing and downsizing
You can move to a larger or smaller unit, swap to a different unit (for example closer to the loading dock), or transfer to another location at any time, subject to availability. Transfers mid-lease are prorated: you pay the new rate from the transfer date. Ask us which units are available and we will reserve one for you.

## Moving out and final inspection
Give us notice of your planned move-out date. On move-out day, empty and sweep the unit, remove your lock, and a staff member will do a short inspection. Your final charge is prorated to your move-out date.

## Climate-controlled units
Climate-controlled units are kept between 55°F and 80°F all year. You can upgrade to a climate-controlled unit, subject to availability; the rate difference is shown before you confirm.

## Pest control
The facility is treated for pests every month by a licensed contractor. If you see signs of pests in or near your unit, report it and we will arrange an inspection.

## Next steps: Billing & Autopay Dispute
Keep your latest statement handy. The billing team can see your charges and payment history, so there is no need to send card details. Never share your full card number in chat.

## Next steps: Gate Access & Lockout
If you are at the facility now and cannot get in, use the call box at the main gate during office hours. Please do not try to force a gate or lock.

## Next steps: Unit Transfer & Reservation Change
Let us know the unit size, location and date you need. A reservation is held for 7 days once confirmed.

## Next steps: Damage & Insurance Claim
If it is safe, take photos of the affected items and the unit, and move undamaged items away from the problem. Do not throw anything away until the claim has been reviewed. If there is active water or fire, call emergency services first.

## Next steps: Delinquency & Auction Notice
Contact us as soon as possible. We can review your balance with you and set up a payment plan to keep your unit in good standing.
